Chicken rice bowl

PortionsPlan says 1 · amounts update below 3

Ingredientsfor 3 portions
Chicken breast495 kcal450 gJasmine rice, cooked891 kcal690 gBroccoli153 kcal450 gOlive oil372 kcal3 tbsp · 42 gSoy sauce9 kcal3 tsp · 15 g
How to make it5 steps · about 25 min
1Cook enough rice for 690 g cooked — about 240 g dry in 600 ml water.12 min
2Cut 450 g chicken breast into strips. Season with salt and pepper.
3Heat 3 tbsp olive oil in a pan. Fry the chicken until golden.7 min
4Steam 450 g broccoli until bright green and tender.5 min
5Split rice, chicken and broccoli into 3 bowls. Drizzle each with 1 tsp soy sauce.
